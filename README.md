***
### Homework 38.1 Working with the online store cart
***
This project provides a simple console application for managing products and shopping carts. It supports CRUD operations for products and allows users to add and remove products from the cart.

**Features**
1. Product Class: Represents products with `id`, `name`, and `price`.
2. ProductRepository: Manages products with CRUD operations.
3. Cart Class: Allows adding/removing products by ID.
4. Console Application: Interact with products and cart through commands.

**Usage**
1. Add a Product: Enter the product ID to add it to your cart. 
2. Remove a Product: Enter the product ID to remove it from your cart.
3. View Cart**: See the contents of your cart.
